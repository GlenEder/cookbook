## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion, diced
- 14 oz can full-fat coconut milk
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes, oil drained
- 1 Tbsp italian seasoning
- 28 oz can diced tomatoes
- 2 - 19 oz cans white beans
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 2 cups spinach, chopped
- 1 cup of rotini

## Instructions

Sauté the diced onion in a frying pan till translucent. While that is cooking down, blend the coconut milk, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, and seasonings together until smooth. Combine the blended mixture and sautéed onions in the slow cooker with the beans, diced tomatoes, and broth. Cook on low for 4-6 hours (high for 2-3 alternatively). With 30 minutes left on the timer, add the pasta to the pot and stir in. Then with 5 minutes remaining, add the spinach. Salt and pepper to taste once done as always. Top with grated parm and fresh basil if available.
