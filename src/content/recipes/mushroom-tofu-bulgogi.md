## Ingredients

- 1 block of extra firm tofu
- 2 Tbsp of pure maple syrup, no Aunt Jemima corn syrup garbage allowed here
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp sesame seed oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ lb mushrooms of choice
- Green onions, for garnish (required)
- Sesame seeds, for garnish (optional)
- Chive sprouts for garnish (heavily optional)

## Instructions

Cut the tofu into bite size cubes and pan fry with a neutral, high temp oil. Once browned on all sides, discard excess oil properly. Add mushrooms, garlic, whites of the onion, and salt. Simmer for a couple minutes until tender. Add in syrup, soy sauce, and sesame oil and reduce. Serve over rice or with some noodles, I really don’t care, just garnish with the thinly sliced green onions and sesame seeds.
