## Ingredients

- 16 oz of super-firm tofu (sub extra-firm, but press out all liquids aggressively)
- ⅓ cup mayo
- 2 Tbsp sriracha
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 scallions finely sliced
- 2 Tbsp shredded carrot

## Instructions

Shred the super-firm tofu with a cheese grater (or food processor if you’re cool like that). Mix thoroughly with the rest of the ingredients. Adjust sriracha for heat, and soy sauce for saltiness. Refrigerate for an hour to let tofu take on more of the flavors for best results.
