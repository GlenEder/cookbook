## Ingredients

- ¾ cup of water
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 3 sprigs of rosemary
- 1 cup honey

## Instructions

Bring the water to a boil and then add the rosemary and cinnamon sticks. Let simmer for 5 minutes. Add the honey, stirring till completely dissolved. Remove from heat and let cool. Strain and store for up to 1 month.
