## Ingredients

- 2 avocados
- 1 jalapeno
- ⅓ cup plain greek yogurt
- 1 Tbsp garlic powder
- 1 Tbsp onion powder
- ~¼ cup water or more

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a high speed blender, minus the water. Blend together, adding water slowly till desired thickness is reached. Salt and pepper to taste.
