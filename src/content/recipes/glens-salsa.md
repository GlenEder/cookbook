## Ingredients

- 28 oz can of whole peeled tomatoes (buy good canned tomatoes, it really shows)
- 14.5 oz can of diced tomatoes
- 1 large onion, peeled and quartered
- 1 bundle of cilantro, washed and ends chopped off
- 1-2 jalapenos (hotter peppers can be used if desired)
- A generous helping of minced garlic

Forgive me for the following measurements, I truly haven’t mesaused the spices in years. When I say lap, I mean a tapping of the spice bottle all the way around the center pillar of the food processor. These are baselines and should not be read like the ten commandments; like I said, forgive me here.

- ½  lap mustard powder
- 2 laps cumin
- 2 laps chill powder
- 2 laps black pepper
- 2 laps salt

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a food processor (or blender if you have to) and pulse until desired consistency is reached. Taste with chips as to not over salt and adjust flavor levels to your own heart's content.
