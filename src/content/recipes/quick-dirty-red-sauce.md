## Ingredients

- 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes
- 14 oz can of diced tomatoes
- 1 red onion
- Minced garlic
- ~2 Tbsp of tomato paste
- Crushed red pepper flakes
- Basil
- Oregano
- Cumin

## Instructions

Sweat out a finely diced onion in a large saucepan. Add minced garlic to taste and cook till fragrant. Add tomato paste and cook for 1-2 minutes. Add the rest of the ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce to a light simmer once boiling and cook for at least 15 minutes and up to 1 hour to develop more flavor. Salt and pepper to taste. A small amount of sugar can be added to sweeten the sauce if used for a pizza sauce.
