## Ingredients

- ½ - 1 lb paneer, cubed (can be substituted with chicken if you eat that)
- ¼-½ stick of butter
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp kashmiri chili powder
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- Pinch of cayenne (optional)
- ¼ cup greek yogurt
- 1 can of diced tomatoes
- 1 chopped onion
- ginger, grated
- garlic, grated
- ½ cup heavy cream
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

Combine paneer with spices, yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, and ginger in a bowl. Thoroughly mix and allow some time to marinate, up to overnight. Brown the marinated paneer in a medium saucepan with some butter and set to the side. In the same pan add the rest of the butter along with the chopped onion, diced tomatoes,and more garlic and ginger. Cook until tender and spice with the same spices from the marinade. Add heavy cream and blend to desired consistency. Combine with the paneer and simmer for a few more minutes. Serve over rice, garnished with fresh cilantro.
