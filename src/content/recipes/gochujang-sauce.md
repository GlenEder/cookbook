## Ingredients

- ¼ cup Gochujang
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp ketchup
- 2 Tbsp mirin
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 Tbsp minced garlic
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- Splash of water

## Instructions

Mix together in sauce pan and reduce over low heat till desired consistency
