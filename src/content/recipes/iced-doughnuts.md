## Ingredients

### Dough

- 340g all purpose flour
- ¾ cup of whole milk (I’m using whole oat milk)
- 6g instant yeast
- 36g sugar
- 1 egg + 1 egg yolk
- 3g salt
- 60g unsalted butter (½ stick)
- oil for frying

### Icing

- 2 cups powdered sugar
- ½ cup cereal of choice (frosted flakes)
- ½ cup milk

## Instructions

Heat the milk to yeast’s required temp and stir in the yeast and sugar. Separately, whisk together the eggs and extra yolk. Add yeast mixture to the stand mixer, begin to mix and add in the whisked eggs and salt. Combine flour and salt in another bowl, then slowly add to the mixer. Once all drys have been mixed in thoroughly, add in the softened butter and continue mixing until homogenized. This will be a wetter sticky dough so don’t panic. Pour out and let rest in a greased bowl for 1 hour (or doubled in size).

Degas and roll out on a floured surface into a rectangle ¾” thick. Cut out doughnuts with ring mold. Combine excess dough and let rest for 5 minutes before rerolling out. Heat oil bath to 350 and fry for ~1 min on each side. Let dry on a wire rack.

While cooling, combine milk and cereal and let sit for about 10 minutes. Strain out the milk. Add powdered sugar to a mixing bowl and slowly add the cereal milk slowly till a thick icing forms. Dunk the top half of the donuts in the icing and cover as desired with fresh cereal.
