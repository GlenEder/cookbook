## Ingredients

- 10-12 oz bag of riced cauliflower (3 cups of florets)
- 1½ cups cooked quinoa and/or brown rice (8.5 oz package of precooked blend)
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs
- 2 eggs

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 400. Cook rice/cauliflower as required. Pulse rice and cauliflower in a food processor if not already riced. Combine all ingredients in a mixing bowl, salt and pepper to taste. Roll into small balls and space evenly across the baking sheet. Brush tops with oil and bake for 20 minutes.
