## Ingredients

- 3 cups all purpose flour
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1½ tsp baking soda
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 2¼ cups milk (whole preferred)
- ¼ cup vegetable oil (or any neutral oil)
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 3 large eggs

## Instructions

Combine all the drys in a large mixing bowl and whisk together to ensure even distribution. In another bowl, mix the wet ingredients together thoroughly. Pour all the contents of the wet bowl into the large dry bowl and mix just until all drys are coated; do NOT over mix. Lumps are expected and required. Let the batter rest for 10-15 minutes while you preheat a skillet or pan. Cook till light brown on both sides in butter.
