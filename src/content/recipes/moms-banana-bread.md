## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup butter (or mayo if you’re Glen and can’t read your mother’s handwriting)
- 2 large eggs
- 2 cups AP flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- add ½ teaspoon baking powder
- 3 ripe af bananas

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350; mix all ingredients together until well combined. Bake for 1 hour.
