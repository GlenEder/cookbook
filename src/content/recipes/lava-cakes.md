## Ingredients

- 115g unsalted butter
- 165g bittersweet chocolate
- 2 whole eggs
- 2 additional egg yolks
- 1½ Tbsp vanilla extract
- 43g granulated sugar
- 21g brown sugar
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 20g all purpose flour

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 450. Grease 4 ramekins and lightly dust with some flour, ensure excess flour is dumped out. Fill a saucepan with half an inch of water and bring to a light simmer. In a separate bowl that fits within the saucepan, without touching the water, add the chocolate (chopped if needed) and cold butter. Heat slowly by placing the bowl in the pot with boiling water, stirring occasionally. In another bowl, combine the eggs and yolks and beat thoroughly together. Once doubled-ish in volume, constantly stir while slowly adding the sugars, salt, and vanilla extract. Mix that with the chocolate once fully melted. Add in the all purpose flour and mix. Divide the batter across the ramekins and cook in the oven for 10-11 mins. They should be done on the outside, but undercooked inside, hence the lava part. Invert onto a plate and dust with powdered sugar for that extra visual umami.
