## Ingredients

- ½ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup apple cider vinegar
- ⅓ cup ketchup
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 tsp water
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

Combine water and cornstarch to form the slurry. Combine all other ingredients into another bowl and mix together. Slowly mix in slurry, reduce over heat if required.
