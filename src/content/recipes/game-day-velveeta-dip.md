## Ingredients

- 32 oz block of original Velveeta cheese
- ~6 oz Franks Red Hot Sauce
- 2 10oz cans of Rotel
- ~1 cup of milk
- 1 lb of your choice of sausage (optional)

## Instructions

If including the sausage, veg or not, start by browning this thoroughly in a large skillet. Once browned, add the whole block of velveeta and begin to chop it up in the pan. Add milk slowly to help melt the cheese and reach desired viscosity. Do this on a lower temp so as to not burn the cheese and ruin the whole dish. Add both cans of Rotel and mix thoroughly. Then, tasting as you go, add the franks red hot to your level of heat tolerance and salt and pepper to taste likewise.
