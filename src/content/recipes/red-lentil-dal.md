## Ingredients

- 1 large red onion, diced
- 1-2 green chilies
- 1 Tbsp chopped ginger
- 4-6 cloves of garlic, chopped
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 3 cups of vegetable stock
- 15 oz can of diced tomatoes
- 1½ cups of red lentils
- Cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

In a deep pot or dutch oven, sauté onions and add the garlic, ginger, and all spices once translucent. Brown lightly from there and add the can of tomatoes once browned. Scrape up the fond using the acidic tomatoes and then pour in the vegetable stock. Using an immersion blender (or whatever blender device you have) blend till desired consistency. Add the red lentils and thoroughly stir and bring mixture to a low boil. Reduce to a low simmer and cover with a lid, stirring every 10ish minutes. Cook for 30 minutes in total and serve with rice, garnishing with cilantro.
