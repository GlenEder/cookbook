## Ingredients

- 150g white granulated sugar
- 150g cold water
- 3-5 sprigs of fresh rosemary

## Instructions

Same as above Simple Syrup, just add the rosemary sprigs to the mixture. Strain out the cooked rosemary when bottling, and optionally add a fresh one to the storage container. Store for only 1-2 weeks in the fridge though as the addition of the greens greatly reduces the shelf life.
