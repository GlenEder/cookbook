## Ingredients

- 150g granulated white sugar
- 150g cold water

You can also do this ratio by volume instead, i.e. 1 cup of sugar to 1 cup of water

## Instructions

Combine in a sauce pan and bring to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat and let cool. PS, you can boil this longer to extract different flavors; the solution will start to change to a dark yellow the longer you cook it for. Just don’t go crazy, and as always, taste as you go. Store for up to 1-2 months in the fridge.
