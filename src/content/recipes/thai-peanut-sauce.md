## Ingredients

- ½ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup water
- ¼ cup tamari soy sauce (or regular soy sauce)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar (or lime juice)
- 3 Tbsp brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon sriracha

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a bowl thoroughly. Pour over rice noodles, veggies, and tofu. Suggested vegetables: red pepper, carrot, cucumber, edamame, etc. Garnish with cilantro, green onion, and chopped peanuts for more of deez nutz
