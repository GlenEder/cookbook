## Ingredients

- Diced tomatoes
- 3 parts extra virgin olive oil (or your oil of choice)
- 2 parts balsamic vinegar
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- 10 basil leaves, diced
- Salt and pepper
- Bread, baguette works great

## Instructions

In a bowl combine all ingredients together (excluding the bread dummy), mixing thoroughly. Let the mixture marinate for 10-15 minutes for best results. Toast baguette for extra crunchiness, and serve pre-topped or on the side if not serving immediately.
