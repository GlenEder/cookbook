## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion, diced
- 45g unsalted butter
- 3-4 cloves of garlic, minced
- Pinch of red pepper flakes, adjusted to spice level tolerance
- 6 oz can tomato paste
- 28 oz can whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 pint heavy cream
- 100g parmigiano reggiano
- 1.5 oz vodka (surprisingly optional, also this equates to one shots worth)
- 1 lb of pasta (your choice here, but should be good at scooping up the sauce)

## Instructions

Start a pot of water for the pasta, and cook 2-3 minutes shy of preferred doneness while preparing the sauce. Sauté the onions in the butter along with the garlic and pepper flakes. Once cooked down, roughly 6-10 minutes, add the whole peeled tomatoes and paste and optional vodka. Sauté for another 10 minutes. Bring the heat to low and add the heavy cream. It should be a pink-ish color, not that iconic orange yet so don’t stress. Transfer to a blender and add the cheese. Blend until smooth, salt and pepper to taste. Transfer back to the original pan adding the pasta to the sauce. Add little bits of the pasta water till sauce hits the desired consistency.
