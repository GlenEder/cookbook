## Ingredients

- 700ml vegetable broth (more may be needed if your risotto is super thirsty)
- 400g short grain rice
- 2 ish cups of chopped mushrooms
- 1 diced onion
- 1 lb of asparagus chopped into 1” pieces
- 2 Tbsp minced garlic (or as much as your breath can take)
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp nutritional yeast (optional)

## Instructions

Saute mushrooms, onions, and garlic in a medium sized saucepan. Then add the rice and toast for 1-2 minutes till ‘nutty’. Add ~100 ml of broth at a time to the rice cooking on a medium low heat. Stir the risotto every few minutes, adding more broth when the rice has absorbed most of the currently added broth. Repeat until the rice is tender; taste the damn risotto, do not assume it is ready. Add asparagus and cook for another 3-5 minutes. Stir in lemon juice and optional Nuch; Salt and pepper to taste.
