## Ingredients

- 16 oz of mac pasta
- Vegan Cheese
- ½ cup panko
- 2 Tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

Combine sauce with cooked pasta and serve, heating in pan if required. If you’re a psycho that enjoys breading on mac & cheese then preheat your oven to 350. Toss the panko with the oil. Place the mac & cheese in a casserole dish and cover evenly with the panko mixture. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until golden brown.
