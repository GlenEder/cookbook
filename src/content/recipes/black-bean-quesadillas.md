## Ingredients

- 2 large flour tortillas
- ¼ - ⅓ cup of chunky salsa
- 1 can of black beans
- ½ onion sliced
- ½ of a lime’s juice
- Taco seasoning
- Nutritional yeast (optional, but highly recommend)

## Instructions

Heat a 12” skillet with a thin layer of vegetable oil. Combine everything but the tortillas and onion in a food processor (or just mash together in a bowl with a fork). Pulse a couple of times to combine, looking to not make a paste here. Spread mixture out on half the tortilla and top with the onions and any other fillings your heart desires. Toast in the pan till both sides are golden brown. Served best with a side of hot sauce, sour cream, and lettuce.
