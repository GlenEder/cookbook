## Ingredients

- 2 15 oz cans of chickpeas (aka garbanzo beans), washed & drained
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 2 Tbsp parsley, chopped
- 2 Tbsp cilantro, chopped
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp coriander
- 2 Tbsp flour
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients, minus the flour into a food processor. Mix on high speed adding flour little by little till transformed into a pretty thick and dry mixture. Shape into 4 equal sized patties and grill on each side for ~3 minutes. Add salt and pepper to taste
