## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1 Tbsp cornstarch
- 120ml stock (~½ cup)
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 1½ Tbsp rice vinegar
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 inch knob of ginger, minced
- 1-2 tsp of red chili flakes
- Sesame seeds and green onions for garnishing

## Instructions

In a wok (or sauce pan) heat a neutral high temp oil over medium-high heat. Add the garlic, ginger, and chili flakes to the oil and cook until fragrant. Add the rest of the ingredients, minus the cornstarch and thoroughly mix. Lower the heat and on the side combine the  cornstarch with water to make a slurry. While constantly stirring, slowly pour in the slurry until desired thickness is achieved. Kill the heat and toss in any proteins, i.e. tofu cubes, chicken or use however you feel like using it, idc.
