## Ingredients

- 1 (14-18oz) block of extra firm tofu
- ¾ cup water
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ tsp smoke powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- BBQ sauce of choice
- 2 tsp paprika
- Buns & toppings of choice

## Instructions

Cut tofu into 1” cubes. Add all ingredients except for BBQ sauce to tofu and marinate for 2 hrs to overnight. Grill tofu until browned or bake for 30 minutes. Crumble tofu and mix in bbq sauce. Add to rolls and top with a slaw of choice
