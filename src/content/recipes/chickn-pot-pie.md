## Ingredients

- 1 pound chicken alternative
- 2 cups chopped carrots
- 2 cups chopped celery
- 2 cups chopped shallots (or onions)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 5 cloves garlic (minced)
- 2 tsp fresh thyme (chopped)
- 2 tsp fresh sage (chopped)
- 1 sprig of rosemary
- ½ cup unsalted butter
- ½ cup flour
- 1 cup unsweetened non-dairy milk
- 1 Tbsp Mushroom Seasoning (or nutritional yeast)
- 1 package frozen puff pastry (thawed according to package instructions)

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 425. Cook and brown chicken in a large oven safe skillet. Set aside and cook down all veggies except the peas in the same pan using the butter. Add the garlic and all spices once cooked. Slowly add the flour to the veggie mix ensuring all is absorbed before adding more. Once all the flour is absorbed, add the milk and cooked chicken to the pan. Reduce. Take off the heat and top with puff pastry as desired, coating with egg wash. Finish off in the oven for 20-25 minutes. Let set and cool for at least 5 minutes.
