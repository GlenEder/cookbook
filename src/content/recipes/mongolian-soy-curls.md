## Ingredients

- 8 oz of soy curls
- 60ml soy sauce
- 60ml dark soy sauce (can sub for just more soy sauce)
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar
- 60ml water
- 2 Tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 1-2 inches of grated ginger
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- The greens of green onions, cut into 1” segments

## Instructions

Rehydrate soy curls with vegetable broth (or just boiling water). Lightly toss the soy curls in some cornstarch. Pan fry and set to the side. In the same pan, simmer the garlic, ginger, and chili flakes in the leftover oil, adding more if needed. Once fragrant add the rest of the ingredients. Thicken with cornstarch slurry to desired consistency and toss in with the fried soy curls. Serve over rice.
