## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter (softened)
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon baking salt
- 12 oz of semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 375. Mix butter, sugars, and eggs in a mixer. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt to the mixture; this will make a stiff dough. Stir in chocolate chips. Scoop out desired sized cookies onto a baking sheet, leaving 2” between scoops. Bake for ~7 minutes; do not overcook. Remove from the sheet and let cool.
