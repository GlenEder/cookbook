## Ingredients

- 180g water
- 280g bread flour
- 10g instant yeast
- 15g olive oil
- 5g sugar
- 9g salt

## Instructions

Heat water to yeast’s desired temp (110 probably). Combine all ingredients in a mixer with a dough hook attached. Mix on a medium high speed for ~6 minutes or until the dough is no longer sticking to the bowl. Divide dough into 12 equal pieces and roll out into logs and knot; a basic overhand is all that is required. Place into a loaf pan or across a sheet tray (this is untested) and proof for 1hr.

While proofing, make the garlic butter by melting some butter, mixing it with garlic, salt, rosemary, basil, thyme, and whatever else you want honestly. Preheat the oven to 450. Cover the knots with a generous brush of the garlic butter. Optionally, top with parmesan cheese for extra looks and flavor. Cook for 12-15 min or until golden topped. Once out, top again with the butter and more cheese if desired.
