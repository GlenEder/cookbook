## Ingredients

- 1 lb of your favorite vegetarian sausage
- 2 cups white rice
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup diced bell peppers
- 1 cup diced onion
- 1 cup diced celery
- 1 cup diced carrots
- 6 oz can tomato paste
- 4-6 cloves garlic minced
- 1 Tbsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper

## Instructions

Saute the bell peppers, onions, and celery; add garlic once fragrant. Once sautéed, add the rice and tomato paste.  Mix thoroughly and let the rice get a little toasted (don’t burn it here, just a couple minutes for everything to get to know each other). Add the broth along with the sausage. Add seasonings and optional addition of hot sauce. Bring to a boil, covering with a lid once boiled. Reduce heat to low and let simmer for 10-12 minutes.
