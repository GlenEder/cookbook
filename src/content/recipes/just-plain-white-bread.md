## Ingredients

- 175g Water
- 125g Whole Milk (I use whole oat milk, ‘cause lactose)
- 9g active dry yeast
- 440g bread flour
- 9g salt
- 13g sugar

## Instructions

Mix together water and milk and heat to 100 degrees. Add yeast to the mixture and let sit aside for 10min to bloom. In the mixer bowl, combine flour, salt, and sugar and mix together. Once yeast has bloomed, using the dough hook attachment, slowly add the yeast mixture to the drys on low speed. Mix/knead till dough is smooth. Form dough into a ball and place it in a greased bowl; cover and let sit for ~1hr to double in size.

Punch down all the gasses once proofed and roll out into a rectangle the width of your loaf pan. Roll tightly and place in the greased pan. Let proof again for another hour. Preheat the oven to 350 and bake for 30-40 min, aiming for a golden brown roof. Let cool on a drying rack for at least 5 min.
