## Ingredients

- 280g All-purpose flour
- 200g white sugar
- 230g brown sugar (ideally split 50/50 with light and dark brown sugar)
- 3g ginger
- 5g cinnamon
- 4g salt
- 7g baking soda
- 1g nutmeg
- 4 large eggs
- 7g vanilla extract
- 1-¼ cups of vegetable oil
- 250g of shredded carrots
- 8 oz of cream cheese
- 220g of powdered sugar
- ¼ cup of milk

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350. Mix together all dry ingredients except the carrots, cream cheese, and powdered sugar in a large mixing bowl. In another bowl crack and whisk the eggs together, pouring in the vanilla extract. Slowly pour in the vegetable oil while continuously whisking the eggs. Pour in the wets with the drys and mix thoroughly. Add in the carrots and gently fold them in. Pour into a greased pan (lined with parchment paper ideally) and cook for 35-45 minutes. Once done, remove cake from pan and let cool on drying rack. While cooling, whisk together the cream cheese, powdered sugar, and milk to form the frosting. Evenly coat the cake once cooled.
