## Ingredients

- 3½ tsp active dry yeast
- ½ cup + 1 Tbsp white sugar
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 4 large eggs (plus another for egg washes)
- 1 Tbsp salt
- 8 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions

In a bowl, combine the yeast plus the 1 Tbsp of sugar with 415 ml of water (to yeast packages temp rec). In the stand mixer’s bowl, combine the drys and mix together. Add in all the wets. Mix and knead with the dough hook. Knead until the dough no longer sticks to the side. Adjust flour and water as needed; use very small  adjustments. Cover and let rest in a greased bowl for one hour.

Punch down risen dough and let rise again for another half hour. Once re-risen, form the dough into equal parts for braiding, usually 4 or 6 braids. Do the braiding, then cover with an egg wash and let rest on the baking sheet and let rise for an hour. Preheat the oven to 375. Perform one more egg wash and bake for 35-40 minutes. Let cool on a rack before slicing.
