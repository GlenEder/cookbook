## Ingredients

- 1 poblano pepper
- 1 jalapeno pepper
- 1 serrano pepper
- 2 white onions
- 1 lb of beef alternative
- 12 oz tomato paste
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans of kidney beans
- 12 oz beer
- 20 oz vegetable stock
- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper

## Instructions

Brown the beef in a large stock pot. Once browned, add diced vegetables and brown as well. Add the rest of the ingredients and stir well, scraping off the fond from the bottom of the pot. Cook on medium-low heat for 1-1.5 hours. Salt and pepper to taste.
