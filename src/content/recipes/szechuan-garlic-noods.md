## Ingredients

- 1 block of extra firm tofu
- 8 cloves of garlic, diced
- 2” knob of ginger, diced
- 4 green onions, diced
- 2 Tbsp dark soy sauce
- 4 servings worth of noodles, your choice
- 1-2 Tbsp of szechuan chili powder
- 1½ Tbsp of vegan oyster sauce

## Instructions

Bring a pot of water to boil and cook noodles as directed, but pull them out just before fully cooked. Crumble up the block of tofu with a fork. Heat up some oil and add the garlic, ginger, and chili powder. Add tofu crumbles to the mixture and cook until lightly browned. Add the soy sauce, vegan oyster sauce and cook for 3-5 minutes. Use this time to scrape up any fond stuck to the pan at this point. Add the just shy of fully cooked noodles to this and mix. Add green onions. Mix thoroughly and serve with an extra sprinkle of szechuan powder on top. I usually serve this with a side of sauteed broccolini.
