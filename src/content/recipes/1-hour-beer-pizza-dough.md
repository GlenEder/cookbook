## Ingredients

- 8 oz of light beer
- 350g All purpose flour
- 5g salt
- 8g sugar
- 7g instant yeast (one packet)
- 15g olive oil

## Instructions

Heat beer to 98 degrees. Combine all other ingredients and mix together in the mixer’s bowl; mix till well combined. Once beer is heated, slowly stream it into the drys while mixing with a dough hook. Knead for 2min once all together. Split the dough into 2 equal segments and shape into balls. Cover with a damp towel and let sit for 15 minutes.

Roll out the balls to desired pizza size, no larger than 12”. Let rest for another 30 minutes. Use this time to preheat the oven and your stone to 550. After the second rest, top the pizzas with desired toppings and sauces. Shovel into the oven and cook for 6-7 minutes.
