## Ingredients

- 1 block of firm tofu
- 1 head of broccoli
- 1 carrot
- 1 red onion
- 1 lb thin rice noodles
- 2 Tbsp tamarind paste
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 red thai chili pepper (optional)
- 50g bean sprouts
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts
- Cilantro & lime wedges for garnish

## Instructions

Cook the noodles by submerging in hot water for 2-3 mins, stirring to remove excess starch. Prep the sauce by combining the tamarind paste, syrup, soy sauce, and optional pepper. Saute the onions, then add the tofu and broccoli. Finally add the carrots, noodles, chinese chives, bean sprouts, and the sauce. Cook for a few minutes; plate with cilantro and the roasted peanuts as toppings.
