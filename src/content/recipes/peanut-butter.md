## Ingredients

- Unsalted and de-shelled peanuts (~1lb works well for most food processors)
- Sugar
- Salt

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350. Disperse nuts across a sheet pan. Roast in the oven for 10 min or until a light golden color appears. Let cool to holdable temp then add to a food processor. Mix on high for ~10 minutes or until desired consistency is achieved. Add in salt and sugar to taste at the back end of the mixing.
