## Ingredients

- 2 parts mezcal
- 1 part triple sec
- 1 part lime juice
- 1 part simple syrup
- ½  part watermelon liquor

## Instructions

Shake well with ice, strain and pour into copper mule glass. Garnish with a sprig of rosemary and a slice of orange.
