## Ingredients

- 1 block of extra firm tofu
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 3 Tbsp cornstarch
- Gochujang Sauce
- 1-2 heads of broccoli
- 2-3 cups of cooked white rice
- Sesame seeds & green scallions for garnish

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 400. Cut up tofu into bite size pieces and place in a bowl. Coat tofu with the olive oil, add any extra spices here (i.e. salt, pepper, garlic powder). Once thoroughly coated, toss gently with cornstarch to evenly coat all pieces. Line evenly on a baking sheet and cook for 30-40 mins. Use this time to make the Gochujang Sauce and cook the broccoli, steaming is recommended. Once the tofu is done baking, toss tofu with the sauce.
