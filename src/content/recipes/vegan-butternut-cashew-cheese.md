## Ingredients

- 400g (~14oz can) of peeled, cubed, and deseeded butternut squash
- 130g (1 cup) raw cashews
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dijon
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Splash of lemon juice (½Tbsp)
- 500g vegetable broth (or water)
- Nutritional yeast to taste (2-4 tsp)

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees fahrenheit. Evenly spread squash across a baking sheet and drizzle with oil. Bake for ~30 minutes or until easily pierced with a fork. Cover and soak the cashews with hot (just shy of boiling, but NOT boiling) water for 10 minutes. Bring another pot up to boil for cooking the pasta and cook as directed.  Once that is all over, combine all ingredients minus ½ of the liquid in a blender. Blend till desired consistency (will thicken as it cools), salt and pepper to taste.
