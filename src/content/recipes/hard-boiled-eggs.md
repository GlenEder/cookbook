## Ingredients

- Eggs, duh

## Instructions

Place eggs in a cold pan. Cover with enough cold water to cover all the eggs. Bring to a boil and cook for an additional 5 minutes. Remove from the heat and let sit in the hot water for 20 minutes. Meanwhile, prepare a bowl of ice water. Gently dent the eggs with each other to allow the ice water to seep in. Place cooked eggs in the ice water and begin peeling. Don’t allow them to get too cold.
