## Ingredients

- ½ lb of Macaroni
- ½ block of Velveeta cheese
- Whole Milk

## Instructions

Cook macaroni just shy of al dente. Drain macaroni and add cheese to the pot. Fill the pot to cover with milk. Slowly heat the mixture to melt the cheese and serve.
