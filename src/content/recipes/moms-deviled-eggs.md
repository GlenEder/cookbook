## Ingredients

- 10 Large eggs
- 1 Tbsp of Lawry’s Seasoned Salt (Kimberly will have your head if you put more)
- 1¼ cups of Mayo (Kimberly also swears that you must use Hellman’s)
- 3 Tbsp of Yellow Mustard
- Paprika

## Instructions

Hard boil all the eggs. Once boiled, deshell and split in half, separating the yolks into another bowl and the whites into the trays. Crumble the yolks with a fork. Combine seasoned salt, mayo, and yellow mustard with the yolks. Mix together with a hand mixer, adding more mustard to taste. Dollop filling into egg whites. Top with sprinkling of paprika.
