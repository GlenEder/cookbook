## Ingredients

- Russet Potatoes (enough to fill the stand mixer, per Kimberly)
- 16 oz sour cream
- 8 oz cream cheese
- ½ stick butter

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350. Skin and boil the potatoes. Mash the potatoes to desired consistency. Combine the mashed potatoes with sour cream and cream cheese in the mixer. Salt and pepper to taste. Once well combined, pour into a greased casserole dish. Dollop with butter slices and bake for 15 minutes.
