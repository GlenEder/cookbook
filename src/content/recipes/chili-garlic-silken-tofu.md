## Ingredients

- 1 block of soft or silken tofu
- 2 scallions finely sliced
- 1 Tbsp minced garlic
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 Tbsp chili crisp
- 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Instructions

Drain tofu and set on a clean towel to soak up extra moisture while making the sauce. In a small saucepan combine all ingredients except the green parts of the scallions. Cook until bubbles start to form. Add some of the green parts, saving some for garnish. Serve tofu over rice and cover tofu with hot sauce.
