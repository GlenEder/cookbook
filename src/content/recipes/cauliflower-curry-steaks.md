## Ingredients

- 1 large cauliflower head
- 1 large onion
- 1 Tbsp of cashews
- 1 Tbsp garam masala
- 1 Tbsp cumin
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste

## Instructions

Slice the onions and sauté in a large skillet. Add cashews and toast. Once lightly browned, transfer to a small blender and add the spices and tomato paste. Blend thoroughly adding water to get desired thickness for a thick curry, salt and pepper to taste; set to the side. Add high smoke point oil to the pan and preheat. Cut cauliflower head into 1”+ thick slices. Sear in the pan till dark golden brown on both sides. Once both sides are browned, serve with curry dressed on top.
