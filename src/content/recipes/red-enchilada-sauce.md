## Ingredients

- 1 cup white/yellow onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 7 mild dried ancho chilies
- 0-2 arbol chilies (for heat)
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup water
- ¼ cup tomato paste
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Instructions

Saute onions and garlic for ~5 minutes or until browned. Add dried chilies and toast for 2 minutes on each side. Add water and broth to the pan, bring to a boil, and let simmer for 15 minutes. Then add the rest of the ingredients and cook for another 5 minutes. Transfer to a blender and blend until desired consistency. Used for Black Bean & Spinach Enchiladas.
