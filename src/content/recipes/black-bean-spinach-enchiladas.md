## Ingredients

- 2 cups enchilada sauce (see Red Enchilada Sauce)
- 1 red onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ lb of baby spinach
- 1 15 oz can black beans
- 1 cup of Mexican cheese
- 8 8” tortillas
- Cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees. Saute onions in a large pan. Add bell pepper, garlic and cook down, followed by the cumin and spinach. Once the spinach is done, transfer everything to a mixing bowl and combine with the beans, cheese, and half the enchilada sauce. Prepare a 13x9 by putting a small layer of the sauce down. Assemble the enchiladas by spreading roughly a tablespoon of sauce on a tortilla and then ~½ cup of the filling mixture. Roll and place seam side down, rise and repeat for all tortillas. Pour the remaining sauce over the tray and cover with the cheese. Bake for 20 minutes. Top with cilantro.
