## Ingredients

- 4ish cups of cooked rice, day old works best but not required
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 knobs of garlic, diced
- 2 inch knob of ginger, diced
- 1-2 Tbsp of spicy chili oil
- 4-6 eggs
- A handful of green onions
- Soy Sauce
- 12-16 oz of frozen vegetable medley
- 1 block of extra firm tofu

## Instructions

In a large skillet, saute onions. Once translucent, add ginger, garlic, ½ the green onions, and the chili oil. Cook till fragrant. Add cracked eggs and scramble in the mixture. Add all the rice to the pan and mix well. Add soy sauce to the rice and mix thoroughly. Do not over saturate with soy sauce! Once mixed and rice is a light golden color add the frozen vegetable medley and mix throughout. Cover the skillet and let steam for 3-5 minutes, or until all the vegetables are cooked. Meanwhile, chop tofu into bite size cubes and finely slice the remaining green onions. Add tofu and onions to rice once done and serve with Sriracha.
