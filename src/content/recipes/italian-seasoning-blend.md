## Ingredients

- 2 ½ Tbsp dried oregano
- 2 ½ Tbsp dried thyme
- 1 Tbsp dried basil
- 1 Tbsp dried rosemary
- 1 Tbsp dried sage

## Instructions

Mix the spices together and store somewhere, I don’t care where.
