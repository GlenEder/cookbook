## Ingredients

- tofu, diced and pan seared
- sweet corn
- quinoa
- edamame
- Cherry tomatoes, quartered or halved
- Arugula
- Apple cider vinegar dressing

## Instructions

Combine the above ingredients with whatever ratio you prefer.
