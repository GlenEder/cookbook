## Ingredients

- 1 15oz can of chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 15oz can of diced tomatoes (use fresh if you like spending money)
- 1 medium red onion, diced
- 1 large bag of spinach, washed
- 1-2 green chillies (sub with jalapeno or serrano), diced
- A good ‘ol spoonful of garlic, minced
- 1 inch knob of fresh ginger, grated
- 1 Tbsp cumin seeds
- 1 Tbsp fennel seeds
- 1 Tbsp mustard seeds
- 1 Tbsp coriander seeds
- 1 Tbsp garam masala powder
- Optional, but really not, splash of lemon juice

Preface: The spices I use in this dish have never been measured or recorded, proceed at your own risk. Adjust to your personal pallet.

## Instructions

Heat up some oil in a Kadai (or a large skillet). Add the whole seeds to the oil and quickly saute to toast the spices, this should only be for like 30-60 seconds. Add onions and saute till translucent. Then, add ginger, garlic, and the chillies, continuing to saute till fragrant. At this point, add in all the powdered spices. Let them all get to know each other for a bit then add all the spinach and cook down till wilted. Transfer into a blender (or food processor for a slightly chunkier texture) and blend till smooth. In the same pan, add some more oil and then lightly brown the chickpeas. Add some extra garam masala here to give the peas an extra kick of flavor. Once browned, pour in the blended mixture and wash the blender with a small splash of water to get everything out and back into the pan. Add the splash of lemon juice to brighten up the dish. Salt and pepper to taste, serve over rice.
